## 3. Donor B Implementations Rejected for Safety Violations

During code review of `./Samanvay.AI`, several implementations were identified as dangerous and were deliberately rejected to protect A's safety invariants:
1. **Donor B's `machine_learning/safety.py` & `backend/app/matching/*`**: Accepted higher pressure classes and upgraded metallurgy as matching items and allowed missing attributes to pass. **Rejected**: Invariant S1/S2 states identity is not substitution; higher classes and upgraded alloys are distinct physical assets and receive -1.0.
2. **Donor B's Carbon Equivalent (`IIW_CE`) Formula**: Treated missing elements (Mn, Cr, Mo, V, Ni, Cu) as `0.0`. **Rejected**: Invariant S15 requires missing elements to raise `CE_INCOMPLETE` flag rather than reporting artificially compliant values.
3. **Donor B's Grade Limits Shortcut**: Applied generic F316 limits to F316L (accepting carbon up to 0.080% for low-carbon 316L). **Rejected**: Invariant S15 enforces strict ASTM A182 limits (F316L max carbon is 0.030%).
4. **Donor B's Explainability Messages**: Celebrated `✓ Pressure class upgrade`. **Rejected**: Pressure upgrades are unsafe substitutions in piping and pressure vessels.
5. **Donor B's Ephemeral Active Learning Queue**: Used an in-memory `deque` which discarded steward corrections upon shutdown. **Rejected**: Replaced with thread-safe atomic disk persistence in `data/active_learning_queue.jsonl`.
